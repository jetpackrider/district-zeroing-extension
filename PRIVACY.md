# Privacy policy

*Zero Deviation browser extension. Last updated November 24, 2026.*

Zero Deviation reads the district plan you have open on davesredistricting.org and works out which census blocks to move. All of that happens inside your browser.

**What is collected: nothing.** Your plan, your district assignments and your Dave's Redistricting account details are never sent to the developer or to anyone else. The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no accounts.

**The one kind of network request it makes.** The first time you work on a state, the extension downloads that state's public census data files from `jetpackrider.github.io` (GitHub Pages). Like any web download, this shows GitHub your IP address and which state's files you asked for. It says nothing about your plan. GitHub's handling of that request is covered by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

**What is stored on your device.** The downloaded data files, and a few settings: whether "Paint steps for me" is on, and which districts you locked on which map. Removing the extension removes all of it.

**What it changes.** Nothing, unless you turn on "Paint steps for me" and click its button, which paints one walkthrough step in the map you have open, through Dave's Redistricting's own paint tools.

**Optional basemap.** The fallback mini-map draws blocks on a plain background and loads no map tiles. A basemap can only be added by changing a setting by hand, and the tile server you choose would then see which area the map shows.

Zero Deviation is not affiliated with or endorsed by Dave's Redistricting.

Questions: [open an issue](https://github.com/jetpackrider/district-zeroing-extension/issues).
